The MIT license for Webbit source code does not grant rights to Ravitz Computers trademarks, logos, the Vinny character, medallion, or related artwork. Those assets remain the property of Ravitz Computers and are used in Webbit with the owner's authorization.

Included assets are copied unchanged from https://github.com/Ravitz-Computers/HARE at commit 41b5615d84825597d81f0dda5ee3859cda4aecd8. See assets/provenance.json for source paths and SHA-256 hashes. The inventory covers the five original PNGs and four original dark-theme SVGs (hello, investigating, love and logo-no-words) in public/hare. SVG paths, fills, gradients and viewBox are retained.

assets/webbit-icon.svg places the full-detail logo-no-words vector in a padded, transparent square without stretching. src-tauri/icons contains application icons generated from that wrapper.
